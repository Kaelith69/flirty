## What Is Pluto?

**Pluto** is a cross-platform Flutter app that uses Google's Gemini 1.5 Flash AI to generate
either:

- 💘 **Cupid Mode** — a flirty, romantic pickup line in response to whatever text you give it
- 🧠 **G Man Mode** — a clever, high-intellect witty reply

You type a message, pick your persona, hit send, and the AI does the heavy lifting. It's
essentially an AI-powered social confidence boost in app form.
